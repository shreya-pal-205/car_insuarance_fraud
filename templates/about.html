About This Project 🚗💡

Welcome to our Car Insurance Fraud Detection system! Insurance fraud costs companies billions of dollars every year. With the power of AI & Machine Learning, we aim to detect fake or exaggerated insurance claims efficiently.

This platform helps insurance providers:

  • ✅ Identify fraudulent claims early to prevent financial loss
  • ✅ Process genuine claims faster and more accurately
  • ✅ Improve trust between customers and companies
  • ✅ Automate manual review tasks for claim assessments

Our AI model analyzes multiple features of a claim, such as:

  • Driver history and previous claims
  • Accident details and damages
  • Claim amounts and patterns
  • Suspicious trends and anomalies

🌟 With this project, we aim to bring transparency, fairness, and efficiency to the insurance industry. AI is not here to replace humans but to assist and empower better decision-making. 🚀
